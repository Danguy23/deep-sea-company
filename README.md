# deep-sea-company
Generated by [Rojo](https://github.com/rojo-rbx/rojo) 7.7.0.

## Getting Started
To build the place from scratch, use:

```bash
rojo build -o "deep-sea-company.rbxlx"
```

Next, open `deep-sea-company.rbxlx` in Roblox Studio and start the Rojo server:

```bash
rojo serve
```

For more help, check out [the Rojo documentation](https://rojo.space/docs).

## Milestone 2 underwater look test

Run `rojo serve`, connect Studio's Rojo plugin to the existing salvage place,
stop any old Play session, sync, and Play with Output open. The look is applied
on Play, rather than baked into the edit-time scene. `ServerScriptService.Server`
starts its `AtmosphereService` and `LightService` children, then starts the unchanged
`RunService`. AtmosphereService owns global visuals and runs only once. LightService
owns the optional directional flashlight. No movement or creature is added.

1. Filter Output for `[Atmosphere]`. Expect exactly one
   `Underwater look applied | fog 4-60 studs; clock 0; brightness 0` per Play.
   If the authored Lighting has an Atmosphere object, a warning explains its
   removal during Play: Roblox Atmosphere overrides linear fog. Stop restores
   the authored object. No persistent scene edits or MCP script writes are needed.
2. Leave the flashlight off and look around at ground level, toward distant
   geometry, and up. Floor and crates within a few studs should be readable, with
   a dim blue-green cast; geometry fades into dark teal from 4 to 60 studs.
   Distant walls and the baseplate
   edge should disappear into murk. There should be no sun, moon, or stars.
   Outside your immediate surroundings it should remain dark with the flashlight
   off. Existing scene lights remain intact. Check low and high graphics quality.
3. In Play's server Explorer, select Lighting. Expect FogStart=4, FogEnd=60,
   FogColor RGB(6,20,24), Ambient and OutdoorAmbient RGB(38,56,62), ClockTime=0,
   Brightness=0, and both EnvironmentDiffuseScale and EnvironmentSpecularScale=0.
   There should be no Atmosphere child; the Sky has CelestialBodiesShown=false
   and StarCount=0. Inspect the client Lighting too to confirm the applied look.
4. Walk outside SafeZone for five seconds during Dive: oxygen should fall from
   100 to about 75, with HP still 100. Return to refill at 20/sec. Approach a
   crate within 10 studs and press E; carry speed remains 12.8 for default Weight
   4. Press G in SafeZone; the same Value banks and hands clear. Complete a
   200-value quota and expect Return -> Payout -> Dive with the next quota 250.
   The fog stays unchanged across run transitions and respawns. The full
   Milestone 1 success and failure checks below still apply.
5. Stop Play, adjust `Config.Underwater`, sync, and Play again. `FogStart` and
   `ViewDistance` control the fog fade range/density: a shorter range gives
   denser water. `ViewDistance` is visual occlusion, not a rendering or streaming
   optimization. `TintColor` controls the murk; `AmbientColor` controls nearby
   readability. The remaining lighting and sky tunables live alongside them.

### Flashlight test

1. Filter Output for `[Light]`. On spawn expect
   `<player>: flashlight ready OFF | range 50 studs; angle 55; brightness 5`.
   Press F to toggle: expect ON/OFF in server Output and a visible beam when on.
   There is no flashlight button or status row in the HUD. Typing F in chat
   must not toggle the light; E and G still pick up and drop/deposit loot.
2. With the flashlight on, aim at a crate or wall roughly 20-40 studs away.
   It should be much easier to identify inside the cool-colored cone; objects to
   the side should stay dark. Turn the camera left/right and look up/down: the
   cone follows where you look, including in first person. Test with carried loot
   as well. Fog still obscures everything at 60 studs; the light improves effective
   visibility within the fog rather than changing the global fog cutoff.
3. In Play's server Explorer, inspect the character's Head:
   `FlashlightAttachment.Flashlight` is a SpotLight with Range=50, Angle=55,
   Brightness=5, Shadows=true, and Enabled matching the server toggle log. There is exactly one
   attachment/light per living character. Shadow appearance depends on Studio's
   lighting settings and graphics quality. No physical flashlight Tool is needed.
4. Reset Character with the flashlight on. The old light disappears and the new
   character receives one flashlight, switched off. Toggle it again. Completing
   a quota with a living character keeps the light's chosen state; death/respawn
   resets it. The Milestone 1 oxygen, quota, loot, and movement values are unchanged.

All flashlight tunables live in `Config.Flashlight`. LightService is a distinct
system because player light ownership and spawn/death cleanup do not belong in
the global atmosphere or oxygen/loot services. The existing client handles the
F key and samples camera direction as input intent at most every 0.1 seconds
while on. The server validates and rate-limits requests, owns Enabled and beam
settings, and anchors the light to the player's Head; the client never supplies
a light position. The light is limited by cone and range, with no battery system
or threat added. Its server-owned Enabled state is available for a future threat.

Local verification: Rojo build includes LightService and both flashlight
RemoteEvents. Visual tuning and the interaction checks above require Studio Play.

### SafeZone marker test

`StarterPlayerScripts.Client` displays a small teal home icon outside the sub
during Dive. The existing OxygenUpdated snapshot supplies the SafeZone position
and in-sub status from the server; no duplicate zone lookup or new service is
needed. Marker size, colors, and screen padding live in `Config.SafeZoneMarker`.

1. Sync and Play. Expect the original four-row HUD, with no flashlight UI. F
   still toggles the light and `[Light]` still logs ON/OFF.
2. During Dive, leave SafeZone and face the sub. The home icon floats over its
   SafeZone even through dark fog. Turn away: it stays within the screen margins
   with a small arrow pointing toward the sub. Check left/right, up/down, and
   directly behind you. Follow the marker back; it disappears when the server
   reports `entered sub` and the HUD shows `In sub`.
3. Resize the Studio viewport. The marker should remain within the margins and
   above the bottom HUD. It hides during Lobby/Return/Payout, death, or a missing
   SafeZone, then returns outside the sub on the next Dive. Reset Character to
   check that only one marker persists.
4. Temporarily rename SafeZone before Play: expect the existing `[Oxygen] MISSING`
   warning and no marker. Restore the name to recover. Navigation is display
   only: walking speed, oxygen drain/refill, pickup, deposits, and quota behavior
   remain unchanged.

## Milestone 1 full-loop test

Run `rojo serve` and connect the Studio Rojo plugin to the existing place containing
`Workspace.Submarine.SafeZone` and Parts tagged `Loot` with numeric `Value`
attributes. A fresh Rojo build does not include this hand-built scene. Stop the
old Play session, sync the source files, then Play with Output open.

`ServerScriptService.Server` starts its `RunService` child, which starts the existing
quota, loot, and oxygen services. `StarterPlayerScripts.Client` displays their
server snapshots. All game logic lives under `src/`; no Studio code paste is needed.

### Startup and oxygen

1. Filter Output for `[Run]`. Expect `Lobby | waiting for a living player`, followed
   by `Lobby -> Dive | dive 1; banked 0 / 200; oxygen 100` after 3 seconds with a
   living character present. Oxygen and pickup are paused before Dive.
2. Expect `[Loot] Fresh dive: respawned 10 scene loot Parts` for the current test
   scene (otherwise the number of tagged scene Parts). HUD shows empty hands,
   `Banked: 0 / 200`, full oxygen, and `HP 100/100` at the start of Dive.
3. Outside SafeZone, oxygen drains at 5/sec: about 75 after 5 seconds and 50 after
   10 seconds. Inside SafeZone it refills at 20/sec up to 100. Filter `[Oxygen]`
   for `outside`, `entered sub`, and periodic values. Refill between loot trips.

### Successful payout and next Dive

1. Approach loot within 10 studs and press E. The carried Part follows you; default
   Weight 4 changes WalkSpeed from 16 to 12.8. HUD shows its Value.
2. Press G outside SafeZone: it drops, speed returns to 16, and bank stays unchanged.
   Pick it up again, enter SafeZone, and press G: the Part disappears, hands empty,
   speed returns to 16, and `[Quota]` logs the deposit. Repeated G adds no credit.
3. In the original scene, deposit Loot5 (100), Loot4 (75), and Loot2 (25), refilling
   between trips. Totals should be 100/200, 175/200, then 200/200. If your scene has
   different Values, deposit any combination reaching at least 200.
4. Expect exactly one `QUOTA MET` and these `[Run]` transitions:
   `Dive -> Return | quota met`, then after 1 second
   `Return -> Payout | banked 200 / 200; SUCCESS`, then after 2 seconds
   `Payout -> Dive | dive 2; banked 0 / 250; oxygen 100`.
   An overshoot is also success; its actual banked value appears in Payout.
5. During Return/Payout, oxygen pauses and pickups/deposits are disabled. In the
   next Dive, HUD shows 0/250, full oxygen, 100 HP, and empty hands. All original tagged
   Parts reappear with their original attributes and newly scattered positions;
   dropped/moved leftovers disappear. No extra Parts or pickup prompts accumulate.
6. For a second success, Loot2 + Loot3 + Loot4 + Loot5 total 250. The next quota
   is 300. The current ten scene Parts total 975; higher-value loot is farther
   out. Refill fully between trips and plan for the slower haul home.

### Failure, unbanked loot loss, and automatic reset

1. During any Dive, pick up a Part and stay outside until oxygen reaches zero
   (about 20 seconds from full). Do not bank enough to meet quota.
2. At zero oxygen, expect `[Oxygen] ... OUT OF AIR` and HP draining at 20/sec.
   The character remains able to move and carry loot. After another five seconds
   without air, expect `[Oxygen] ... DOWNED at HP 0/100`, followed by
   `[Run] Dive -> Return | all players downed/out of air`,
   `Return -> Payout | banked <actual> / <quota>; FAILED`, and
   `Payout -> GameOver | quota missed; resetting run`.
   The character dies. Carried loot earns no credit; hands clear and carry speed
   is restored. Run-end carry cleanup logs lost unbanked loot; if death cleanup
   already dropped it, it remains disabled and is cleared before the next Dive.
3. After 2 seconds of GameOver, expect `[Quota] Reset. Banked: 0 / 200` and
   `[Run] GameOver -> Lobby | run reset; waiting for crew`.
   After the character respawns and the 3-second lobby delay, expect
   `Lobby -> Dive | dive 1; banked 0 / 200; oxygen 100`.
   No Stop/Play is needed. Fresh loot is restored, oxygen drains again outside,
   and pickup/deposit works again.
4. Repeat failure after a successful Dive to verify the raised quota also resets
   to 200. Reset Character during Dive to verify a dead single-player crew ends
   the Dive as well. Each Dive ends once, even if end signals overlap.

### Scene warnings and configuration

Stop, temporarily rename SafeZone, and Play: expect a loud `[Oxygen] MISSING`
warning, a `SafeZone missing` HUD during Dive, and paused oxygen. Restore the name
and updates resume. Invalid/missing loot Values warn and disable pickup. No tagged
loot or non-Archivable loot warns because there is no valid respawn template.

LootService captures the original tagged scene Parts once when it starts, before
adding runtime prompts. Stop/Play after editing the scene to capture a new layout.
Runtime-added loot is cleared on the next Dive and is not added to the original set.
All tunables live in `src/shared/Config.luau`: LobbyDelay=3, ReturnDelay=1,
PayoutDelay=2, GameOverDelay=2, StartingQuota=200, and QuotaIncrease=50.

## Loot range and drowning checks

Save the Studio place after scene edits: the ten tagged crates are physical scene
data, so Rojo builds do not include them. Each has a `SpawnRadiusFraction` attribute
and numeric `Value`. The fractions are 0.25, 0.35, 0.45, 0.55, 0.65, 0.8, 0.9,
0.96, 1.03, and 1.07. Values rise from 10 to 180 with distance. Parts without the
fraction retain their authored positions.

`ServerScriptService.Server` runs OxygenService and LootService through RunService;
`StarterPlayerScripts.Client` displays oxygen and HP. On Play, filter Output for
`[Loot] Spawn`: expect estimated trips of roughly 5–22 seconds, calculated using
20 seconds of oxygen, the item's carrying penalty, and one second for interaction.
Spawns are capped to leave two seconds before drowning death. They vary in angle
and distance each Dive, sit on level ground, and must have a clear route/sightline
from the sub. A failed placement warns loudly instead of spawning hidden loot.

With a full tank, stay outside: HP should stay at 100 for 20 seconds, then reach
about 80, 60, 40, 20, and 0 over the next five seconds. At about 22 seconds, return
to SafeZone: expect the `rescued in sub` log, about 60 HP held steady, and oxygen
refilling at 20/sec. HP resets to 100 on a new Dive or respawn. Return/Payout pause
oxygen and drowning. Approach Loot10, press E, return and press G: expect carrying
speed 12.8, a visible crate/HUD, and a deposit of 180.

between 5.5 and 21.7 seconds; oxygen checks passed partial-tick damage, five-second
drowning, rescue at 60 HP, pause, HP reset, and suppression of passive regeneration.
Live Play confirmed ten spawns, drowning/run-end logs, visibility and pickup of
Loot10, carrying speed 12.8, and its 180-value deposit.

Local verification: Rojo build succeeds; server sources parse in Studio; a
